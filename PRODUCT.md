## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML/CSS/JS, no framework, deployed to Vercel. Brief asked for "no complications"; the site is three pages with video, so a build step buys nothing. (inferred from brief)

## Users

Primary: marketing managers and founders of small and mid-size brands (Poland, Ukraine, wider EU) who need UGC video and are choosing a creator or someone to coordinate several creators. They arrive from Instagram or a DM link, usually on a phone, and decide in under a minute whether to write. (inferred from site content and Instagram-story screenshot)

Secondary: UGC creators considering joining the coordination network. (inferred)

## Product Purpose

Personal site of Kateryna Ts. (katyats.com), Warsaw. Two offers under one name:

1. **UGC Creator & Model** — she films the content herself: testimonials, unboxings, tutorials, story-driven clips, voice-over videos, model shoots, product and lifestyle photography, branded-content adaptation, content strategy (storyboarding, scripting, editing). Up to three revisions included.
2. **UGC Coordination for brands** — she finds, briefs, manages and QA-checks other creators, one point of contact for the brand.

Success = a brand writes to ugc.by.katets@gmail.com or Telegram after seeing the work.

## Positioning

She is both the creator and the coordinator: a brand can start with one person and scale to many without changing the contact. Communication in English, Polish, Ukrainian and Russian. Neighbouring UGC creators sell only themselves; agencies sell only coordination.

## Operating Context

Visitors mostly come from Instagram (@katyatsss_ for the creator side, @ugc.by.katets for coordination) and Telegram (@kkkkkkkkts creator, @kate_ugc coordination). Brands evaluate on a phone, then forward the link to a colleague on desktop. Contact is by email and Telegram; there is no form, no booking, no pricing on the site.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Three routes: `/` (chooser), `/creator/` (portfolio), `/coordination/` (service for brands).
- Portfolio: 12 vertical 9:16 videos with project titles (Cycling app, Real estate app, An event, Massage studio, Hotel, Beauty clinic, Photo artworks, Dance studio, Boxing school, Flower shop, A coffee shop, Beauty salon). Silent muted previews plus full clip with sound in a player.
- Coordination stats (real, from the site): 15+ UGC projects delivered, 500+ creators in the network, €7K+ ad spend managed, 3+ years in marketing & UGC.
- Coordination services: creator search, communication & coordination, campaign management, content control, revisions, one point of contact. Process: tell us what you need → we find creators → we coordinate everything → you receive content.
- Languages: English, Polish, Ukrainian, Russian.
- Location: Warsaw, Poland.
- No CMS, no backend. Undecided: pricing (not shown), client logos (none on hand).

## Product Principles

- The work is the argument: video leads, prose follows.
- One name, two doors: the creator/coordination split must be obvious in the first second.
- Everything reachable from a phone with one thumb; contact never more than one tap away.
- Red is identity, not decoration: commit to it at page scale.
